# CEO Gotchas

## Treating CXX As Commands

CEO must not call `/coo`, `/cto`, or similar slash commands. CXX routing is agent/skill invocation.

## Over-broad Meeting

Do not summon every CXX for every mission by default. Ask only the roles needed for the decision.

## Owner As Progress Pump

Do not ask the Owner whether to continue, start the next step, hire a worker, choose between internal options, or verify routine technical behavior. Decide internally, record the rationale, and keep routing. If a decision is too ambiguous for CEO alone, convene the relevant CXX agents and decide from their recommendations.
