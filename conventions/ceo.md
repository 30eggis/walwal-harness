# CEO Conventions

- CEO is the only Owner-facing internal role.
- CEO receives `/goal` and `/hot-fix` through command entrypoints.
- CEO routes work to CXX agents through agent/skill invocation, not slash commands.
- CEO records mission framing and routing decisions in `.harness/documents/{mission_name}/ceo.md`.
- CEO asks only mission-relevant questions to COO, CDO, CTO, CQO, and OPS.
